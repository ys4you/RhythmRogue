# RhythmRogue Playtest (Solo / Remote)

> You are playing on your own. No call, no walkthrough, no one watching. That is on purpose.
> Copy this file (or paste it somewhere you can type) and rename it like `2026-06-13_alex.md`.

## Read this first (takes 20 seconds)

- Just dive in and play a run. If you die, start another. Play for roughly 15 to 25 minutes, or until you have had enough.
- We are NOT going to tell you how the game works. Fumbling around is exactly what we want to see, so do not go looking for instructions.
- While you play, only use **Section B (bugs)** below. Try not to read Section C onwards until you have finished, so the questions do not colour how you play.
- There are no wrong answers. Blunt and negative feedback is the most useful kind. If something annoyed you, say so.
- Short answers are fine, and skip anything that does not apply.
- If you get fully stuck and cannot continue at all, note it in Section B and restart.
- The run's seed code is shown on the map screen and the end-of-run summary. If you can, copy it into Section G so we can replay your exact run.
