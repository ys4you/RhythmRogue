## Dev takeaways (added after, not part of the form)

Tester profile: casual-ish (sometimes rhythm games, sometimes roguelikes), on speakers, ~17 min, many retries, stuck on the 2nd enemy.

### Confirmed fixed (no recurrence under heavy retrying)
This session doubles as a real-player smoke test of the post-Playtest-1 fixes:
- **No damage after restart:** she died and retried repeatedly and kept dying on enemy 2, so damage clearly persists across restarts. The Playtest-1 invincibility bug did not reappear.
- **Notes popping mid-screen at start:** none reported; notes were "easy to follow."
- **Misses registering too late:** misses showed at the line and felt fair.
- **Font readability:** "nothing hard to read" (Playtest-1's font complaint did not recur).
- **Hold notes:** worked fine (no slider-feedback issue this session).

### #1 issue: early difficulty cliff gates the whole game (confirmed, both testers)
- Enemy 1 easy, enemy 2 a wall ("immediately knew I was cooked"). She could not pass node 2 in 17 minutes, so she never reached the shop, rest, events, relics-in-practice, or the boss.
- Playtest 1 reported the same shape (Cultist easy, then a hard wall at Zealot).
- Net effect: a casual player experiences ~2 nodes of a multi-node game. The entire roguelike loop sits behind one steep early jump.
- This is tuning (early enemy BPM / pattern density / HP), not a feature. The position of the wall shifts with player skill, but the flat-then-cliff shape is consistent across both testers.

### Repeated secondary signals
- **Enemy / guard layer is invisible.** She thought the right side of the screen was "herself," never mentioned the guard, and was "too focused on myself to watch the opponent." Playtest 1: the enemy "might as well not be there." The guard mechanic is not landing for casual players.
- **Relics not understood.** "Saw them, no idea what they were." Same as Playtest 1.
- **Buried settings go unused.** She found the notes "a bit fast" but never touched the scroll-speed option she had seen. Players will not self-serve buried options. (Design note for calibration: prompt it on first launch, do not bury it, or it will go unused the same way.)
- **Map commitment unclear first time.** Did not realise a path could not be undone; learned it by run 2.
- **Shop discoverability** was her #1 change request, partly because she never progressed far enough to reach a shop node.

### Positives
- 4/5, "would play again for sure."
- Core loop (mash to the beat) understood instantly; arrows clear from the on-screen prompts.
- Timing felt in time on speakers with no calibration.
- Hit detection and misses felt fair.
- Her best moment was the "aha" of understanding the systems (routes, icons).

### Top 3 to act on
1. Smooth the early difficulty ramp (enemy 1 -> 2 -> 3) so a sometimes-player can clear node 2 and actually see the rest of the loop.
2. Make the enemy / guard layer legible (players do not perceive the enemy as a threat or notice the guard at all).
3. Surface relic effects (a pickup or first-relic nudge) and make node types / the shop readable on the map.
