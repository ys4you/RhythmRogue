# Commands & Skills

Quick reference for all available skills and commands in this vault.

## Skills (in 05 Skills/)

- **session-wrap:** at the end of a session, read what changed in git and update the vault (status, shipped systems, codebase map, decisions, session history). Say "wrap up".
- **weekly-update:** interview me and update CLAUDE.md (Weekly Update, goal, Current Status), plus an optional weekly review in `07 Iteration Logs`.
- **pixel-art-conversion:** turn a high-res or AI-generated image into a clean, palette-locked pixel-art sprite. Ships `pixelify.py`.

## Templates

- `02 Playtesting/Templates/_TEMPLATE_SOLO.md`: form for testers playing on their own.
- `02 Playtesting/Templates/_TEMPLATE.md`: notes for an in-person session you watch.

## Commands

- **RhythmRogue > Build Playtest** (Unity menu): one-click Windows development build, named after version, date and commit, zipped and ready to send. Output in `Builds/Playtest/`.
- **Session start check** (automatic in Claude Code): tells Claude when the vault is behind git, so it offers a wrap.
